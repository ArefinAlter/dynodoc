This computer's key

A signing key lets Dynodoc show that a change was made on this computer. Its private part is encrypted and kept by your operating system (Windows data protection, the macOS Keychain or the Linux Secret Service). It is never shown and never sent anywhere. Your system may ask you to approve access to its key store.
