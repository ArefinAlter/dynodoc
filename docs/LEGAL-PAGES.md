# Privacy and terms drafts

The public pages are `/privacy` and `/terms`. Both are linked from the homepage
footer and sign-in page, use the shared marketing layout and include accessible
section navigation. They are explicitly drafts, marked `noindex`, dated 19
September 2026 and not represented as adopted agreements.

The owner has been asked for the legal operator, public legal/privacy email,
mailing address and governing jurisdiction. No details, regulator, legal basis,
liability cap or forum have been fabricated. To finalize the pages, the operator
also needs to confirm hosting and subprocessor locations, applicable processing
arrangements, retention periods and an operational rights-request channel.
Appropriate legal review depends on that jurisdiction and service arrangement.

The implementation-informed disclosures cover:

- Google identity scopes, Resend delivery and first-party authentication cookies.
- Original uploads, snapshots, member roles, private drafts and retained content
  history; archiving or removing current text is not permanent erasure.
- ODK/Kobo tokens used for current requests, retained imported content, and the
  difference between API snapshots and file-only compatibility.
- Current 14-day rolling hosted database backups, with no automatic primary-data
  expiry or self-service erasure feature.
- External image requests from user-authored documents, administrator access,
  security limitations, research responsibilities and conditional statutory rights.
- Content ownership, the limited service permission, separate AGPL code licensing,
  import limitations, acceptable use, pilot availability and account closure.

The account form links to these pages for information. This change does not add
an acceptance checkbox or claim to record agreement to unfinished terms.

The text is original and grounded in the application's current behavior. Provider
policies are linked directly; Dynodoc does not make commitments on their behalf.

## Approved live-data erasure policy (21 September 2026)

The owner approved decision 005: administrator-confirmed whole-document erasure
removes live content/history/files/drafts/public copies, retaining a minimal audit
receipt. Account erasure removes the profile/access/private drafts and preserves
shared contributions under a deleted-user identity. Existing backups expire under
the current retention schedule. Off-site backup is deferred by the owner. The
operator identity/address/jurisdiction remain missing; the public legal pages stay
marked as drafts. This is a record of product behavior and owner direction, not
legal approval of the policy.
