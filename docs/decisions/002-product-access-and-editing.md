# Product access and editing expansion

The owner's expanded product request authorizes hierarchical workspaces, recoverable
deletion, administrator lifecycle actions, revocable public snapshots, richer editors,
Google Forms and LaTeX interchange, onboarding, accessible marketing and product pages.
This extends decision 001. The semantic event log, stable identities and existing
research data remain protected. No existing document is published or deleted by deployment.

Organizations contain teams; teams and folders contain folders and documents. Roles
inherit downward, with the strongest applicable grant winning. A narrower grant does
not remove inherited access. Owners/managers administer the hierarchy; editors edit;
reviewers propose/comment; viewers read. Document ownership is distinct from editing.
Public links expose explicitly published snapshots, excluding private comments, audit
history and original uploads. Owners/managers can revoke links. New edits require a
newly published snapshot. Trash blocks ordinary reads/writes and public links while
retaining the recoverable history. Removing an account disables login, existing sessions
and future event delivery; it does not claim permanent erasure of attributed history.

The feature roadmap must distinguish available, limited/interchange and planned work.
RAG learning tools, agentic document work, study galleries, statistical validation,
field implementation and advanced geospatial workflows are planned capabilities, not
shipped claims. External research describes problems and practices, not measured
Dynodoc productivity or reproducibility improvements. Full Microsoft/Google feature
and file-fidelity parity requires explicit acceptance coverage, not a blanket claim.
